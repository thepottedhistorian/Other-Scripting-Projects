1. First-Time Setup & Permissions

Step A: Authorizing the Spreadsheet Scripts One-Time Step

Before using the menu tools or Web App for the first time, grant permission for the workbook to manage your tabs and Drive photo folders:

  1. In the top Google Sheets toolbar, click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🏥 Run System Health Check.
  2. When the Authorization Required window pops up, click OK / Continue and choose your Google account.
  3. If a warning screen appears saying "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced (bottom-left) and then click Go to Jenny's Orchid Management System (unsafe).
  4. Click Allow on the permissions screen.
2. Deploying & Launching the Web App Dashboard on Your PC

Step B: Activate Your Personal Web App URL One-Time Setup

Your workbook includes a built-in dark-mode dashboard (Jenny's Potted Ledger) for viewing photo galleries, filtering your collection, and logging care on your PC or phone. Follow these steps once on your computer to turn it on:

  1. In Google Sheets, click Extensions > Apps Script in the top menu bar (this opens the script editor in a new browser tab).
  2. In the top-right corner of the Apps Script window, click the blue Deploy button and choose New deployment.
  3. Next to "Select type", click the small gear icon ⚙️ and choose Web app.
  4. Fill in the three fields as follows:
    • Description: Type Jenny's Potted Ledger v1
    • Execute as: Select Me (your email address)
    • Who has access: Select Only myself
  5. Click the blue Deploy button at the bottom right. (If prompted to authorize access, follow the same Advanced > Allow steps from Section 1.)
  6. Once the deployment succeeds, copy your Web app URL (ending in /exec), return to Google Sheets, click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🔗 Set Web App URL, and paste it in!
How to Open It Anytime:
• From Google Sheets: Click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🌐 Open Web App Dashboard.
• Bookmark / Desktop Shortcut on Your PC: Once the Web App opens in Chrome or Edge, press Ctrl + D (or Cmd + D on Mac) to bookmark it—or click the browser's ⋮ (three dots) > Cast, save, and share > Create shortcut to pin Jenny's Potted Ledger directly to your PC desktop or taskbar!

4. Setting Up & Using Google Drive Photo Folders

Automatic Folder Creation & Photo Syncing Google Drive

Your workbook links every orchid to its own dedicated photo folder inside a master Orchid Photos folder in your Google Drive:

  1. Create Your Folders: Click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🖼️ Photo Archive & Drive > 📁 Create / Sync All Drive Photo Folders.
  2. What It Builds: The script creates a main Orchid Photos folder in your Google Drive, creates a numbered subfolder for every orchid (e.g., 1 - Phalaenopsis 'Jenny's First Orchid'), and places a clickable link in cell K3 on each orchid's sheet.
  3. Uploading Photos: Open any orchid's numbered tab and click 📁 Open Drive Photo Folder ↗ in cell K3 (or click + Attach Photo inside the Web App). Drag and drop your photos directly into that plant's folder.
  4. Updating Photo Counts: After uploading new photos to Drive, run 📁 Create / Sync All Drive Photo Folders (or open the orchid in the Web App) to refresh the total photo count (Cell K4) and latest upload date (Cell K5).
5. Adding a New Orchid (Two-Step Workflow)

Onboarding a New Specimen Step-by-Step

You can add orchids either through the Web App's New Orchid button or directly in Google Sheets using the two-step menu workflow:

  1. Step 1 — Provision Row: Click 🌸 Orchid Tools > ✨ 1. Provision New Row. The script finds the next available ID number (e.g., 2), writes it into Column A of Inventory, and places your cursor in Column B.
  2. Step 2 — Enter Details: Type in the orchid's Orchid Name (Col B), Genus (Col C), Acquisition Date (Col I), care preferences, and Repot Every frequency (Col T).
  3. Step 3 — Finalize & Link: Keep your cursor anywhere on that new row, then click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🔗 2. Finalize & Link Ledger.
What happens automatically: The system creates a new numbered tab for that orchid, hyperlinks its ID in Inventory, calculates its first Next Repot Due date, adds a row in Maintenance Log, and creates its Google Drive photo folder.
6. Logging Watering & Fertilizing

Method A: Logging a Single Orchid Quick Entry

  1. Open the Maintenance Log tab and locate the orchid's row.
  2. (Optional) Type fertilizer or care notes into Column E (Notes) before choosing the watering method.
  3. Click the dropdown in Column C (Method) and select Soak, Mist, Flush, Pour, or Spot Watering.
  4. Column D immediately stamps the current date and time, and the entry is copied into that orchid's personal Watering Log (Columns L:N).

Method B: Watering Multiple Orchids at Once Bulk Dialog

  1. On the Maintenance Log tab, highlight the rows of the orchids you watered (hold Ctrl on Windows or Cmd on Mac to select multiple non-adjacent rows).
  2. Click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🚿 Watering & Maintenance > Log Bulk Watering (Selected Rows).
  3. Verify the row numbers in the popup box (click the green refresh button if you change your selection), pick your Watering Method, type optional Notes, and click Log Maintenance.
7. Tracking Blooms & Bloom Durations

Automatic Bloom Cycle Pairing Lifecycle

  1. Starting a Bloom: On Inventory (or inside the Web App Passport), change In Bloom to In Bloom. The script sets Last Bloom Date to today, turns Cell D5 on the orchid's sheet green (🌸 In Bloom), and starts a new row in its Bloom Log (Cell A20+).
  2. Ending a Bloom: When the flowers drop, change status back to Not in Bloom. The script records the end date in Column B of the Bloom Log and calculates the total bloom duration (e.g., 2 months 14 days) in Column C.
  3. Spikes & Buds: You can also set status to In Spike (🌱) or In Bud (🌼) to update the status badge without starting the open-bloom timer yet.
8. Repotting & Stoplight Alerts

Recording a Repot & Updating Due Dates Maintenance

  1. Go to Maintenance Log, type any potting media notes into Column I, and enter the repot date in Column H (Last Repot Date).
  2. The script logs the repot on the orchid's personal sheet (Columns I:J), adds the upper end of its Repot Every frequency (e.g., 1-2 years adds 24 months), and updates Next Repot Due across Maintenance Log, Inventory, and Cell D10 of the orchid's sheet.
  3. To review or recalculate all repot dates at once, click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 📅 Repotting Tools > 👁️ Preview Repot Calculations.

Stoplight Color Key (Inventory Column A & Specimen Cell D10):

  • Green: On schedule (more than 30 days until repot is due).
  • Amber: Upcoming (due within the next 30 days).
  • Red: Overdue (past the Next Repot Due date).
9. Archiving Orchids & Helpful Utilities

Retiring Orchids & Workbook Shortcuts Management

  1. Archiving a Specimen: Click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 📦 Archive Orchid, enter the orchid's ID number, and enter a final status (such as Gifted, Rehomed, or Died). Its summary and total collection age are saved in Archived Orchids, its row in Inventory is hidden, and its tab is recycled as a hidden TMP_ sheet for future orchids.
  2. Quick Jump Sidebar: Click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🧭 Open Navigation Sidebar to search by orchid name or ID number and jump straight to its tab.
  3. Excel Backup: Click 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🛠️ System Formatting > 📊 Export Clean XLSX to Excel to download a clean Excel copy of your workbook.
  4. Database Checkup: Run 🌸 Orchid Tools > 🏥 Run System Health Check anytime to confirm all active orchids have matching ledger tabs and valid dates.
Important Rule: Never right-click and delete numbered orchid tabs manually—always use Archive Orchid so the tab is properly recycled without breaking formulas.
